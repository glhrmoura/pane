# Pane main window

Scope: the main window (Folha, Catálogo, Guardadas, Arrancadas), detail sheet, settings and menu bar. Visitor mode: Experience. Native macOS, light and dark.

Audience and job: general Mac users who want the desktop to keep changing to something beautiful with no effort. Primary action: turn the page (apply a new photograph now) and keep automatic rotation running.

Constraints: photographs are Wikimedia Commons featured pictures; author and license stay visible in detail. Portuguese UI. Both appearances.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The window is a wall calendar for the desktop: every photograph is a page, and the next change is already marked on the grid. It refuses the sidebar-plus-thumbnail-grid gallery every wallpaper app ships.

OWN-WORLD: Cool white coated paper (charcoal board in dark), black ink, one ultramarine ink reserved for "now/next". Square-cut photographs with paper margins, no rounded tiles. Compressed black calendar numerals as display voice, plain sans for captions, tabular figures for times. Wire binding across the top edge. All colour lives inside photographs.

STORY: The user sees what is on the desk now, when it will change, and turns the page with one click; browsing the catalogue is choosing which pages go into the calendar.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Binding wire across the top; the current photograph fills the upper ~58% edge to edge inside the paper margin, credit line under it; below, huge compressed numerals give the next change time beside a day grid of change slots, past slots holding tiny thumbnails, next slot ringed in ultramarine. Primary action "Virar a folha" sits under the numerals.

FORM: Wall photo calendar, position 4 of 7 on the ordered list; seed:8287f8ca207368f9. Raises: colour confined to photographs (lexicon); one strongest emphasis at a time (understory); sections slide as sheets over one page (Rietveld).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: turning the page. The current sheet lifts and slides up off the binding while the new photograph settles in beneath.
